# Spot Blacks & Graphic Black-and-White Shading

## 1. Direct tutorials — Spotting Blacks and Comic Inking

| Resource | Format | Cost | Focus | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [How to "Spot Blacks" in Your Comic Book Art – Emma Kubert](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ba0YuVnFKio) | YouTube video | Free | Dedicated demonstration of spotting blacks in comic art | **Excellent — direct match** |
| [Inking Basics – Sequential Artists Workshop](https://sequentialartistsworkshop.thinkific.com/courses/Inking-Basics) | Structured course | Paid; some previews | Dedicated module on spotting blacks and final inks, with an accompanying exercise called "The Fly" | **Excellent — dedicated lesson** |
| [Inking Superhero Comics – Art Rocket](https://www.clipstudio.net/how-to-draw/archives/159080) | Article | Free | Light direction, line weight, pushing spotted blacks, traditional and digital inking | **Excellent** |
| [Inking Tutorial Part II – Tom Richmond](https://www.tomrichmond.com/inking-tutorial-part-ii/22/08/2006/) | Article with illustrations | Free | Deliberate placement of solid blacks to establish depth, mass, lighting, contrast, and focal points | **Excellent** |
| [Essential Digital Inking Techniques – Brett Parson](https://www.truegrittexturesupply.com/blogs/news/comic-inking-tutorial) | Illustrated tutorial | Free | Part 5 specifically covers spotting blacks and adding shadows; includes a full comic-cover workflow | **Excellent** |
| [Three Comic Inking Tips – Scott Drummond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6YeUPzl-N1A) | YouTube video | Free | At 5:41, discusses using more spot blacks; also covers linework and avoiding excessive hatching | **Very good** |
| [Comics & Sequential Art – The Drawing Path](https://thedrawingpath.com/comics.html) | Structured online lesson | Free | Spotting blacks, line weight, brush inking, crosshatching, and full-page inking exercises | **Very good** |
| [Inking & Panels – CUNY Lecture](https://openlab.citytech.cuny.edu/comd3513-sp22/files/2022/02/WK2-Inking-Panels_small.pdf) | Lecture PDF | Free | Explicitly discusses Mike Mignola, spot blacks, visual focus, negative space, and balancing black areas | **Excellent — particularly relevant to Mignola** |
| [Comic Book Inking: How Do They Do It?](https://iosuaillustraties.wordpress.com/2016/03/01/1121/) | Article | Free | Explains how solid blacks create solidity, frame subjects, and direct the viewer's attention; references Mignola and Frank Miller | **Very good** |
| [Creating Dynamic Black and White Illustrations](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/2818) | Illustrated tutorial | Free | Complete black-and-white illustration workflow, including a dedicated section on spotting blacks | **Very good** |
| [Improve and Understanding Inking Techniques](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/3899) | Illustrated tutorial | Free | Line weight, ambient occlusion, solid shading, hatching, and crosshatching | **Good — broader inking instruction** |

## 2. Graphic composition — Notan and light/dark design

These resources are less about comic inking and more about understanding how to make black and white shapes work together.

| Resource | Format | Cost | Focus | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Exploring Composition through Shape and Notan – Mitchell Albala](https://mitchalbala.com/video-exploring-composition-though-shape-notan/) | Video lesson | Free | Arranging black and white shapes to create strong compositions | **Excellent** |
| [Rivers of Light: An Introduction to Notan](https://annacariensatelier.com/blog/rivers-of-light-and-shadow-an-introduction-to-notan) | Article and exercises | Free | Positive/negative space, two-value compositions, paper-cutting exercises | **Very good** |
| [Composition – Ellen J. McHenry](https://ellenjmchenry.com/downloads/CompDesign.pdf) | Educational PDF | Free | Includes a practical Notan composition exercise using black and white shapes | **Very good** |
| [Notan: The Dark-Light Principle of Design](https://www.google.com/search?q=Notan+The+Dark-Light+Principle+of+Design+Bothwell+Mayfield) – Dorr Bothwell & Marlys Mayfield | Book | Paid | Dedicated study of dark-light relationships, shape, balance, and composition | **Excellent — deeper study** |

## 3. Artists and stylistic studies

These are useful for studying how artists actually employ solid black areas, even when the resource isn't exclusively about Mignola's technique.

| Resource | Format | Cost | Focus | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Hugo Pratt's Pictorial Style – Longstride Illustration](https://longstrideillustration.com/hugo-pratt-pictorial-style/) | Illustrated article | Free | Large solid-black shapes, silhouettes, negative space, minimal rendering, and dramatic contrast | **Excellent — very close stylistically** |
| [How to Render Like Bernie Wrightson](https://longstrideillustration.com/how-to-render-like-bernie-wrightson/) | Illustrated article | Free | Shadow shapes, silhouettes, atmospheric perspective, and line direction | **Very good — some emphasis on detailed rendering** |
| [Mike Mignola Interview – Part Three](https://reverttosaved.com/2008/08/14/an-interview-with-mike-mignola-part-three/) | Interview | Free | Mignola discusses the development of his distinctive visual style and artistic influences | **Useful background** |
| [Do You Speak Comics? Black Ink, White Paper](https://comicskingdom.com/trending/blog/2024/08/05/do-you-speak-comics-part-3-black-ink-white-paper) | Article | Free | Spot blacks, hatching, crosshatching, and other monochrome comic techniques | **Good introduction** |
