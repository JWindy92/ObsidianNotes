# Domain Level Goals
#### Analytical Fluency
> Develop intuition and technical capablity to approach complex problems independently. I want to be able to translate an unfamiliar question into a viable analytical approach, identify the data and methods required, and work through the problem without being constrained by a lack of technical understanding or analytical direction.
#### Technical Judgement and Problem-Solving
> Develop the depth of knowledge to discuss complex data problems intelligently, evaluate alternative approaches, recognize potential pitfalls, and make well-reasoned recommendations about how to achieve a desired result.
#### Professional Data Visualization
> Develop the ability to design and build sophisticated, publication-quality data visualizations comparable to those found in leading online publications and professional presentations. Go beyond conventional charts to incorporate thoughtful visual design, interactivity, animation, vector graphics, and other techniques where appropriate.


# Soft skills

- Know how to investigate a data centric problem
- Know how to reason about the best solution
- Be able to communicate the result effectively and attractively


# Tangible Outcomes

- A portfolio of independent investigations covering several different types of analytical problems, including some you initially didn't know how to approach.

- Several substantial data systems you've designed and implemented, accompanied by documented architectural decisions, performance measurements, and explanations of the trade-offs involved.

- A curated public portfolio of approximately 5–10 exceptional visualizations, with at least some featuring custom interactive or animated elements. Each should be something you'd be comfortable sharing as a representative example of your work.
