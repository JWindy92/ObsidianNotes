[Cult Education Institute](https://culteducation.com/)
- Maintains database of info and articles about many documented cults, religious offshoots, and high control groups

[Freedom of Mind Research Center](https://freedomofmind.com/)
- Founded by [[Steven Hassan]]
	- Former cult member turned cult expert

[International Cultic Studies Association](https://internationalculticstudies.org/) (ICSA)
- In depth research and deep dives into cults and high control groups
- Well regarded
- Doesn't maintain a "list" but has lots of good research
  
[Southern Poverty Law Center](https://www.splcenter.org/hate-map/)
- Tracks hate, anti-government, and extremist groups
