# Sports Betting, Bookmaking & Prediction Markets — Learning Resources

## 1. Foundational Resources

Books and academic material covering bookmaker pricing, betting-market economics, and identifying market inefficiencies.

### Books

- [**The Logic of Sports Betting**](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Logic+of+Sports+Betting) — Ed Miller and Matthew Davidow
    
    - How sportsbooks set lines, respond to betting activity, and manage markets.
        
- [**Squares & Sharps, Suckers & Sharks**](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Squares+Sharps+Suckers+Sharks) — Joseph Buchdahl
    
    - Betting-market structure, participant behavior, and the economics of sports betting.
        
- [**Sharp Sports Betting**](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Sharp+Sports+Betting) — Stanford Wong
    
    - Quantitative betting methods, probability, and evaluating wagers.
        
- [**Monte Carlo or Bust**](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Monte+Carlo+or+Bust+Joseph+Buchdahl) — Joseph Buchdahl
    
    - Probability, simulation, and the mathematics of betting.
        
- [**Handbook of Sports and Lottery Markets**](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Handbook+of+Sports+and+Lottery+Markets)
    
    - Academic reference covering the economics and statistical analysis of betting markets.
        

### Academic Research

- [**Karl Whelan — Economics of Sports Betting**](https://www.karlwhelan.com/BettingMarkets/)
    
    - Course materials on bookmaker margins, market efficiency, and betting economics.
        
- [**Optimal Bookmaking**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2021.03.005)
    
    - Academic research on bookmaker pricing and optimal market-making decisions.
        
- [**Steven Levitt — How Do Markets Function?**](https://www.nber.org/papers/w9422)
    
    - Economic analysis of sports betting markets and how participants interact.
        
- [**Jay Simon — Sports Betting Market Research**](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2022.00456)
    
    - Research related to betting-market efficiency and line movements. Verify the paper's bibliographic details before relying on this reference.

## 2. Ten Quick Reads

Shorter articles introducing practical concepts used to analyze bookmaker behavior and betting markets.

1. [**How Sportsbooks Set Betting Lines**](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/betting/news/how-sportsbooks-set-betting-lines/06bbb4dfdd9b14f04abed737) — Sporting News
    
    - Line setting, sharp action, public betting, juice, and line movement.
        
2. [**How Bookmakers Set Odds**](https://www.sportsbettingmath.org/en/learn/how-bookmakers-set-odds/)
    
    - Bookmaker pricing, odds formation, and the stages involved in setting prices.
        
3. [**Closing Line Value (CLV): Formula and Guide**](https://pinnodds.com/blog/what-is-closing-line-value-clv-guide)
    
    - Comparing your price with the closing market price to evaluate decision quality.
        
4. [**What Line Moves Can Tell Us**](https://www.covers.com/guides/what-line-moves-can-tell-us) — Covers
    
    - Interpreting price changes and potential market signals.
        
5. [**Sharp Money: How to Read Line Shifts**](https://oddsiq.ai/blog/sharp-money-how-to-read-line-shifts)
    
    - How price changes are interpreted as possible evidence of informed betting.
        
6. [**Why the Line Moves: Insight From a Pro**](https://www.sportsbettingdime.com/guides/strategy/why-the-line-moves/) — Sports Betting Dime
    
    - Factors that cause sportsbook odds to change.
        
7. [**How Prediction Markets Work: Order Books & Settlement**](https://marketpredictionguide.com/learn/how-prediction-markets-work/)
    
    - Order books, matching orders, market prices, and settlement mechanics.
        
8. [**How to Read Prediction Market Odds Before You Trade**](https://www.investopedia.com/how-to-read-prediction-market-odds-before-you-trade-11995522) — Investopedia
    
    - Interpreting market prices as implied probabilities.
        
9. [**Line Movement: How to Read, Interpret, and Profit From Shifting Odds**](https://marketmath.io/blog/line-movement)
    
    - Practical analysis of price movement over time.
        
10. [**Closing Line Value Explained**](https://www.sportsbettingmath.org/en/learn/closing-line-value-explained/)
    
    - Why the closing price is commonly used as a benchmark for evaluating bets.
        

**Caution:** These articles vary in rigor. Claims about sharp money, reverse line movement, or profitability are heuristics or hypotheses—not proof of a profitable strategy.

## 3. Ten Free Blogs & Resources to Follow

Resources focused on market analysis, quantitative research, bookmaker behavior, prediction markets, and data tooling. Free-to-read content may coexist with paid products.

1. [**Karl Whelan — Economics of Sports Betting**](https://www.karlwhelan.com/BettingMarkets/)
    
    - Academic material on bookmaker behavior, market economics, and efficiency.
        
    - Best for foundational theory; not a frequently updated blog.
        
2. [**Sports Betting Math**](https://www.sportsbettingmath.org/en/learn/)
    
    - Bookmaker margins, fair odds, removing the vig, market pricing, and statistical biases.
        
3. [**Bet Better — Betting Research & Open Data**](https://virginia826.com/studies/)
    
    - Empirical studies using large odds datasets to investigate margins, price behavior, and market efficiency.
        
4. [**Bankroll Guardian — Research**](https://www.bankrollguardian.com/research)
    
    - Experiments involving bookmaker margins, line shopping, and predictive models, including examples of approaches that fail to produce profits.
        
5. [**OddsPapi Blog**](https://oddspapi.io/blog/)
    
    - Odds feeds, price monitoring, line quality, and comparing sportsbook prices with closing lines.
        
6. [**The Sporting News — Betting**](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/betting/)
    
    - Accessible explanations of line setting, line movement, sharp action, and sportsbook operations.
        
7. [**Previsions — Blog**](https://previsions.ai/blog/market-microstructure-forecasting)
    
    - Prediction markets, forecasting, liquidity, order flow, and market microstructure.
        
8. [**PolyOrderbooks — Blog**](https://polyorderbooks.com/blog)
    
    - Technical work on Polymarket order books, historical data collection, liquidity, and backtesting.
        
9. [**Philipp D. Dubach — Prediction-Market Research**](https://philippdubach.com/posts/the-anatomy-of-a-decentralized-prediction-market-notes-from-the-polymarket-order-book/)
    
    - Technical analysis of prediction-market order-book data and market structure.
        
10. [**Awesome Sports Betting Data**](https://github.com/JacobiusMakes/awesome-sports-betting-data)
    
    - A GitHub collection of datasets, APIs, and tooling for independent analysis.
