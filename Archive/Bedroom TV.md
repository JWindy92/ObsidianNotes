## Mounting Notes

- Mount directly into **at least one wall stud** (preferably two if spacing allows).
- Full-motion mount is preferred for easier cable access and positioning.
- Route power and HDMI through a paintable cable raceway for a clean appearance.
- Leave enough clearance below the TV for dresser decor or future soundbar.
