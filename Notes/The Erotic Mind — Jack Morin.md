# The Erotic Mind — Jack Morin

*The Erotic Mind: Unlocking the Inner Sources of Passion and Fulfillment* (1995) — Jack Morin, psychotherapist. Explores the psychology of erotic attraction, fantasy, and arousal. Argues that erotic patterns are deeply personal and often connected to early emotional experiences.
