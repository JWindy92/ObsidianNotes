# Victorian Flagellation Literature

A surprisingly robust genre of clandestine Victorian-era writing — erotic flagellation literature that circulated privately in 19th century England. Interesting as both a literary curiosity and a window into the sexual repression and shadow culture of the Victorian period.

## Sources
- *The Whippingham Papers* — anonymously published, classic of the genre
