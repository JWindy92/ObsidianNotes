# Anton LaVey & The Satanic Bible

Anton LaVey (1930–1997) — founder of the Church of Satan, author of *The Satanic Bible* (1969). His "Satanism" is largely atheistic and philosophical — closer to Ayn Rand-style individualism and theatricality than literal devil worship.

- *The Satanic Bible* — LaVey (PDF in Drive)
- Michael Aquino — wrote critically about LaVey; former Church of Satan member who split to found the Temple of Set
