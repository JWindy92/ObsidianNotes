# Lukas Moodysson — Director & Poet

Swedish filmmaker and poet. Known for films like *Show Me Love* (1998), *Together* (2000), *Lilya 4-Ever* (2002), and *We Are the Best!* (2013). Work spans warmth and devastating bleakness. Also publishes poetry in Swedish.
